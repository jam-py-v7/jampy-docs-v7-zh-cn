==========================
on_filter_form_close_query
==========================

on_filter_form_close_query(item)

**domain**: client 

**language**: javascript

Description
===========

``on_filter_form_close_query`` 事件由实体项的 
:doc:`close_filter_form <m_close_filter_form>` 方法触发。

``item`` 参数是触发了这个事件的实体项。
