====================
on_filter_form_shown
====================

on_filter_form_shown(item)

**使用范围**: client 

**编程语言**: javascript

**类**: :doc:`Group 类 </refs/client/item_group_api>`

描述说明
===========

The ``on_filter_form_shown`` event is triggered by the 
:doc:`create_filter_form </refs/client/item/m_create_filter_form>`
method of the item when the form has been shown.
在表单显示后， ``on_filter_form_shown`` 事件由实体项的 :doc:`create_filter_form </refs/client/item/m_create_filter_form>` 方法触发。

``item`` 参数是触发事件的实体项。

如果定义了这个事件，会为分组里的每个调用了 :doc:`create_filter_form </refs/client/item/m_create_filter_form>` 方法的实体项触发此事件，
